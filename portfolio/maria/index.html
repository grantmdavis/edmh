Maria

Working with hair as a creative outlet has been a lifelong passion. From teasing my Barbies hair(which was a bad idea, the teasing does not comb out) to sitting in the back of a salon while my mom worked behind the chair, I always loved the vibe. Mostly, I enjoy the connection with strangers that become friends and sharing their special moments. I immediately enrolled in cosmetology school after high school graduation and have been creating ever since. 2021 celebrates my 20th anniversary of working as an independent hairstylist! Also, I believe continued education is the secret to success and have been an Artistic Educator for the brand Martin. This has offered me an opportunity to work with hair designers from coast to coast. Now my journey leads me to my next chapter, sharing more special moments with brides. I look forward to working with brides as they begin their new journey.
